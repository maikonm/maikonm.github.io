## Idle Train ASMR

🚂 Welcome to Idle Train, an addictive strategy and puzzle game that will challenge you to build and manage your very own railway! 🛤️

In Idle Train, you start with a simple train and one carriage on a small loop track. Each time the train passes the padle, you earn money, which can be used to expand your railway network and upgrade your trains.

🎯 Engaging and strategic gameplay:

Add more padles to boost your earnings
Expand your track with stations and forks to pick up and drop off passengers
Merge carriages of the same level to create more advanced and profitable ones

🧩 Puzzle elements to challenge your mind:

Manage the paths of the tracks to meet station demands
Match carriage colors with corresponding passengers to optimize your flow
Test your planning and strategy skills to maximize your earnings

📲 Download Idle Train now and embark on this thrilling railway journey to become the ultimate railway tycoon! 🚆
